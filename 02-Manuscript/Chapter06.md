## 1. PEDAGOGICAL PURPOSE

**Why is this concept being taught here?**
Now that students can identify and use nouns and pronouns, the next logical step is to learn how to describe them. Adjectives add detail, colour, and precision to language, moving students from simple statements to more descriptive and engaging expression.

**What should the learner be able to do with it?**
The learner can:
- Define an adjective and its function.
- Identify adjectives in sentences.
- Differentiate between adjectives of quality, quantity, and number.
- Use appropriate adjectives to describe nouns.

## 5. CONCEPT DISCOVERY (WHAT IS AN ADJECTIVE?)

An **adjective** is a word that describes a noun or a pronoun. It tells us more about a person, place, animal, or thing.

Adjectives often answer questions like:
-   **What kind?** (e.g., a *brave* soldier, a *sunny* day)
-   **How many?** (e.g., *five* pencils, *many* books)
-   **What colour?** (e.g., a *yellow* bus, *brown* hair)
-   **What size?** (e.g., a *big* elephant, a *small* mouse)

*Example:* The **tall** girl is wearing a **blue** dress.

-   `tall` describes the `girl`.
-   `blue` describes the `dress`.

---

## 6. CONCEPT EXPLANATION (KINDS OF ADJECTIVES)

### A. Adjectives of Quality

**Adjectives of Quality** describe the kind, quality, or state of a noun. They answer the question "What kind?"

-   Rohan is an **honest** boy. (What kind of boy?)
-   It was a **delicious** meal. (What kind of meal?)
-   The **clever** fox tricked the crow. (What kind of fox?)

### B. Adjectives of Quantity

**Adjectives of Quantity** tell us how much of a thing there is. They answer the question "How much?" They are used with uncountable nouns.

-   There is **little** water in the jug. (How much water?)
-   He has **enough** money. (How much money?)
-   She ate **some** rice. (How much rice?)

### C. Adjectives of Number

**Adjectives of Number** tell us how many people or things there are, or in what order they stand. They answer the question "How many?" They are used with countable nouns.

-   The hand has **five** fingers. (How many fingers?)
-   There are **several** mistakes in your essay. (How many mistakes?)
-   I will be on the **first** train. (In what order?)

---

## 7. COMMON CONFUSIONS (QUANTITY VS. NUMBER)

Some words like `some`, `all`, `enough`, and `no` can be used as both Adjectives of Quantity and Adjectives of Number.

**How to tell the difference?**
Look at the noun they describe!

-   If the noun is **uncountable**, it is an Adjective of **Quantity**.
-   If the noun is **countable**, it is an Adjective of **Number**.

## 12. LET'S SUMMARISE

In this chapter, we learned that **adjectives** are describing words.

-   **Adjectives of Quality** tell us *what kind* (e.g., `big`, `happy`, `blue`).
-   **Adjectives of Quantity** tell us *how much* (e.g., `some`, `little`, `enough`).
-   **Adjectives of Number** tell us *how many* (e.g., `one`, `five`, `many`).

> **Key Idea:** Adjectives make our sentences more interesting and full of detail!
