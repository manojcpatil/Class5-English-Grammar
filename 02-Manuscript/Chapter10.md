**Ms. Sharma:** "Exactly! The words 'under', 'behind', 'on', and 'by' are very important. They tell us about the position of things or when something needs to happen. These words are called **Prepositions**."

## 5. CONCEPT DISCOVERY (WHAT IS A PREPOSITION?)

A **preposition** is a word that shows the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence.

Prepositions often tell us about:
-   **Position/Place:** *where* something is (e.g., `in`, `on`, `under`)
-   **Time:** *when* something happens (e.g., `at`, `on`, `by`)
-   **Direction/Movement:** *where* something is going (e.g., `to`, `into`, `from`)

*Example:* The book is **on** the table.
-   `on` shows the relationship between `book` and `table`.

---

## 6. CONCEPT EXPLANATION (KINDS OF PREPOSITIONS)

### A. Prepositions of Place

**Prepositions of Place** tell us *where* something is located.

-   The cat is **under** the bed.
-   The picture is **on** the wall.
-   Rohan is sitting **between** Maya and David.

### B. Prepositions of Time

**Prepositions of Time** tell us *when* something happens.

-   We have school **on** Monday.
-   The movie starts **at** 7 o'clock.
-   I will finish my homework **by** evening.

### C. Prepositions of Direction

**Prepositions of Direction** tell us *where* something is moving.

-   The car went **through** the tunnel.
-   Rohan is walking **to** the school.
-   The cat jumped **over** the wall.

## 11. LET'S SUMMARISE

In this chapter, we learned that **prepositions** are connecting words that show relationships.

-   They often tell us about position, time, or direction.

| Preposition Type | What it Tells | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Preposition of Place** | *Where* something is. | The cat is **on** the roof. |
| **Preposition of Time** | *When* something happens. | The class starts **at** 9 AM. |
| **Preposition of Direction** | *Where* something is going. | He walked **to** the store. |

> **Key Idea:** Prepositions help us build clear and detailed sentences by showing how things are related!
