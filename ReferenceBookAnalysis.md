-   **Tense and Verb Forms:** Covers present, past, and future tenses, including time expressions and regular/irregular verb forms.
-   **Active/Passive Voice:** Likely provides an introductory treatment of active and passive voice.
-   **Direct/Reported Speech:** Likely offers an introductory treatment of direct and reported speech.

## 3. WEAKNESSES OF `05.pdf` (Inferred/Hypothesized)

-   **Pedagogical Approach:** May rely heavily on explicit instruction and rote learning, potentially lacking in guided discovery, contextual learning, and application-based tasks.
-   **Lack of Integration:** Grammar concepts might be taught in isolation, with insufficient integration into writing, speaking, and listening activities.
-   **Outdated Content/Examples:** The reference might contain wording, definitions, examples, stories, or illustrations that are outdated, culturally irrelevant, or pedagogically weak for a modern Class 5 learner.
-   **Limited Error Analysis:** May focus on correction without requiring learners to explain the errors, missing an opportunity for deeper understanding.
-   **Visuals:** Could lack purposeful, engaging, and accessible visuals, or rely on generic, unverified images.
-   **Spiral Curriculum:** May teach concepts once and not revisit them systematically in later contexts, leading to a lack of reinforcement.
-   **Assessment:** Could be predominantly objective, lacking in tasks that assess reasoning, application, and creative production.
-   **Terminology:** Definitions might be overly complex or not child-friendly.
-   **Originality:** As a reference, it is explicitly stated that the new book must not copy its content, implying a need for fresh examples, stories, and exercises.

## 4. PROPOSED PEDAGOGICAL ARCHITECTURE (Aligned with `MasterPrompt.md`)

The new textbook will adopt a learner-centric approach, balancing explicit instruction with guided discovery and application. Key elements include:
-   **Learning Progression:** EXPERIENCE → NOTICE → THINK → DISCOVER → NAME → EXPLAIN → PRACTISE → APPLY → CREATE → REFLECT.
-   **Three-Layer Model:** NOTICE (Encounter language) → UNDERSTAND (Learn concept/rule) → USE (Identify, edit, produce, communicate).
-   **Spiral Curriculum:** Concepts will be revisited and reinforced across chapters and activities.
-   **Contextual Learning:** Grammar will be introduced through short stories, conversations, notices, and other real-life contexts.
-   **Integrated Skills:** Strong links between grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, speaking, and listening.
-   **Error Analysis:** "Grammar Detective" tasks will encourage explanation, not just correction.
-   **Purposeful Visuals:** Every visual will have a clear pedagogical objective.
